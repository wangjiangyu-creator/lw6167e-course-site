DRAFT MINISTERIAL DECISION ON ELECTRONIC COMMERCE 
The Ministerial Conference, 
Recalling the "Work Programme on Electronic Commerce" adopted on 25 September 1998 
(WT/L/274), and reaffirming subsequent Ministerial Declarations and Decisions on the Work 
Programme; 
Taking note of the valuable discussions on elect ronic commerce under the auspices of the General 
Council and its relevant subsidiary bodies, and the seminars held by MIKTA, FED and the Afric an 
Group since our last session; 
Recognizing the importance of deepening such discussions under the WTO framework for a better 
understanding of the opportunities and challenges incurred from electronic commerce for the trade 
growth and economic progress of Members at different development stages; 
Decides: 
To continue with the positive work under the Work Programme on Electronic Commerce since our 
last session, based on its existing mandate and guidelines and on the basis of proposals submitted 
by Members in the respective WTO bodies. 
To reiterate the  central role of the General Council  under the Work Programme on  electronic 
commerce, and  considering the cross -cutting nature of E -commerce, to explore appropriate 
mechanisms for necessary focused horizontal discussions in parallel with the respective discussions 
under the General Council a nd its relevant subsidiary bodies, including to reinvigorate the 
Dedicated Discussion of the General Council or at a body to be agreed upon by all Members. 
To instruct the General Council at its first meeting in 2018 to appoint the Chair of the Dedicated 
Discussion, and that a work plan be agreed upon at the first meeting of the Dedicated Discussion. 
To instruct the  Dedicated Discussion to take up trade-related electronic commerce elements 
acceptable to Members, including but not limited to facilitating cro ss-border e -commerce; 
promoting paperless trading; transparency; as well as development and cooperation. 
To instruct the General Council to keep a standing item on its agenda and continue to hold periodic 
reviews in its sessions of July and December 201 8 and July 2019, based on the reports submitted 
by the WTO bodies entrusted with the implementation of the Work Programme  and Dedicated 
Discussion Chair of the General Council , to assess its progress and consider any recommendations

To instruct the General Council to emphasize the development dimension in the Work Programme,
